14. IMPRESSION VS VIEW
==================================================

These MUST be separate.

IMPRESSION:

Product was meaningfully visible in the customer's feed.

VIEW:

Customer intentionally opened the product.

Do NOT call impressions "views."

Do not count:

- hidden products
- below-fold cards never shown
- React rerenders
- admin previews
- merchant self-refresh spam

as genuine customer views.
