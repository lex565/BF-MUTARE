4. ADMIN HIERARCHY — IMPORTANT
============================================================

Musuwo must have TWO levels of platform administration.

LEVEL A — MUSUWO OWNER / ROOT SUPER ADMIN

This is the highest level.

This role represents the actual owner/controller of Musuwo.

There should initially be only ONE primary owner-level account unless the owner explicitly adds another.

Suggested internal role name:

platform_owner

or:

root_super_admin

This account has ultimate platform authority.

LEVEL B — MUSUWO SUPER ADMINS

Allow up to 10 active Super Admin / Reviewer accounts initially.

Suggested role:

super_admin

These people may help review businesses, moderate listings, inspect reports, monitor platform operations, and perform approved administrative work.

THEY MUST NOT HAVE THE SAME POWER AS THE PLATFORM OWNER.
